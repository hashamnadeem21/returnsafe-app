# Delete your ReturnSafe account

You can delete your ReturnSafe account and all of its data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open ReturnSafe and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings › Account › Delete account**.
3. Confirm. If you signed in a while ago, you'll be asked to sign in again first.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

## Without the app

Email **[privacy@getreturnsafe.com]** from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We'll delete the account within [30] days and confirm by email.

If you used ReturnSafe only as a guest (no email), use the in-app option. We have no way to identify a guest account by email.

Pick one and replace this note: (a) show a "Support ID" for guests in Settings and accept it by email here; (b) delete guest accounts automatically after [N] months without use and say so here and in the Privacy Policy; or (c) both. Until then, a guest who has removed the app has no external route.

## What is deleted

- Your account: email, name and sign-in methods.
- All receipts, warranty details and receipt photos.
- Reminder settings, scheduled reminders and push notification tokens.
- Your subscription status record in ReturnSafe.

Encrypted backups containing this data are overwritten within [14] days.

## What is kept

- **Purchase records:** Apple, Google and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) keep records of subscription purchases, as required for tax and accounting.
- **Crash reports and usage events:** these are not linked to your account. Firebase deletes them automatically after up to [90 days] (crash reports) and [14 months] (usage events).

## Your subscription

**Deleting your account does not cancel ReturnSafe Pro.** Cancel it in your store account first:

- **iPhone:** Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
- **Android:** Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

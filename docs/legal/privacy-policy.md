# ReturnSafe Privacy Policy

_Effective date: [Month D, YYYY]_ · See also our [Terms of Use](https://getreturnsafe.com/terms).

ReturnSafe ("ReturnSafe", "we", "us") makes the ReturnSafe app for iPhone and Android. It helps you keep receipts and remember return deadlines and warranty end dates. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. ReturnSafe is offered only in the United States.

**The short version:** we collect what the app needs to keep your receipts and remind you about them. We don't sell your data. We don't use it for advertising and don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and all of your data at any time, in the app or at https://getreturnsafe.com/delete-account.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Account information:** your email address, your name if you provide it, and a sign-in method (email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google). If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's relay address. You can also try the app as a guest without giving us an email; a guest account has only an anonymous identifier.
- **Receipts you add:** the store name, purchase date, amount, items, return window, warranty length, and whether you returned or kept the item.
- **Receipt photos:** photos you take or choose of your receipts.

### Information created when you use the app

- **Reminder settings and device time zone:** we use these to send reminders at the right local time.
- **Push notification token:** an identifier from Apple or Google that lets us send reminders to your device. We store it only while you're signed in and notifications are allowed.
- **Subscription status:** whether you have ReturnSafe Pro, the plan, and its renewal or expiry dates. We receive this from Apple or Google through our subscription provider. We never receive your card or payment details.
- **Crash reports:** device model, operating system version, app version, and the technical details of an error. Crash reports don't contain your receipts, photos, email or name.
- **Product usage events:** a small fixed set of events, such as "receipt added (scanned or manual)", "receipt marked returned", "paywall viewed" and "subscription started". Events contain no receipt details, store names, amounts, text you typed, or your email. They are not linked to your account, and we don't collect your device's advertising identifier.

### Information we don't collect

- We don't collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, browsing history, or health or financial account data.
- We don't use an advertising identifier and we don't track you across apps or websites.
- **Receipt text recognition happens on your device.** The camera image is read by your phone's own text recognition. Only the photo you choose to save and the fields you confirm are uploaded.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide the app: store and sync your receipts and photos across your devices, calculate return deadlines and warranty end dates, and send the reminders you asked for.
- To provide ReturnSafe Pro and check whether your subscription is active.
- To keep the service secure and working: prevent abuse (including checking that requests come from a genuine copy of the app), diagnose crashes, and fix bugs.
- To understand, in aggregate, which features are used, so we can improve the app.
- To respond to you when you contact support.
- To comply with law.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as those terms are defined under California law).

## 3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that process it for us under contract, and only as needed to run ReturnSafe:

| Provider | What it does | Data involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Firebase (Google LLC) | Sign-in, database, photo storage, cloud functions, push notifications, crash reporting (Crashlytics), usage events (Analytics), app integrity (App Check) | Account info, receipts, photos, push token, crash reports, usage events |
| RevenueCat, Inc. | Manages subscriptions and receipts from Apple and Google | Your ReturnSafe user ID and subscription purchase history |
| Apple and Google | Process subscription payments under their own terms; deliver push notifications; Sign in with Apple / Google sign-in when you choose them | As described in their privacy policies |

We may also disclose information:

- if required by law or valid legal process;
- to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or others;
- as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

## 4. How long we keep information

- **Receipts, photos and account details:** kept while your account exists.
- **Account deletion:** when you delete your account, we delete your account, receipts, photos, reminders and push tokens from our live systems right away. Encrypted backups are overwritten within [14] days.
- **Guest accounts:** kept like any other account until you delete it (Settings › Account). A guest account can't be recovered after you sign out or delete the app.
- **Crash reports and usage events:** kept by Firebase for up to [90 days] and [14 months] respectively.
- **Purchase records:** Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep these as required for tax and accounting.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correct:** you can see and edit your receipts and name in the app.
- **Delete:** delete your account in the app (Settings › Account › Delete account) or at https://getreturnsafe.com/delete-account. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings.
- **Notifications:** turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.
- **Photos and camera:** you can deny or revoke camera and photo access at any time; you can still add receipts manually.
- **California residents:** under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use sensitive personal information for purposes that require an opt-out. To make a request, email [privacy@getreturnsafe.com]. We'll verify your request using your account email. An authorized agent may also make a request on your behalf with your written permission.
- **Other states:** residents of states with similar laws (for example Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah and Texas) may make the same requests at the same address. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

## 6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Each account can read only its own data, enforced by server-side rules. Requests must come from a genuine copy of the app. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll notify you as required by law.

## 7. Children

ReturnSafe is not directed to children under 13. You must be at least 13 to create an account. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date and notify you in the app or by email before the changes take effect.

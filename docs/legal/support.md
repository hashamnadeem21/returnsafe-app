# ReturnSafe Support

Need help? Email **[support@getreturnsafe.com]**. We reply within [2 business days]. Please include your phone model and the app version (shown on ReturnSafe's App Store or Google Play page under Version / About this app).

## Common questions

**How do I add a receipt?**
Tap **Scan** to photograph a paper receipt. ReturnSafe reads the store, date and total on your phone; check them and tap Save. You can also tap **Enter manually**.

**How is the return deadline worked out?**
From the purchase date plus the store's usual return window. You can change the window for any receipt. Store policies vary and change (holiday returns, opened electronics and so on), so always check the store's own policy.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
- Make sure notifications are on for ReturnSafe in your phone's Settings.
- Make sure reminders are on in the app (Settings › Reminders).
- On Android, turn off battery optimisation for ReturnSafe if your phone restricts background notifications.

Reminders are sent the day before a deadline, at the time you chose. Pro adds reminders 7 and 3 days before.

**Can I use ReturnSafe offline?**
Yes. You can add and edit receipts offline; they show "Waiting to sync" and upload when you're back online.

**How do I move to a new phone?**
Sign in with the same account. If you used ReturnSafe as a guest, first save your account (Settings › **Save your account to keep your receipts**) so your receipts aren't lost.

**What does Pro include?**
Unlimited receipts, warranty tracking, extra photos per receipt, 7- and 3-day reminders, and export. Prices are shown in the app.

**How do I cancel Pro?**
- **iPhone:** Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › ReturnSafe Pro › Cancel.
- **Android:** Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › ReturnSafe Pro › Cancel.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. For Apple, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, use the Google Play help center (Request a refund).

**I bought Pro but the app still shows Free.**
Open the paywall and tap **Restore purchases** while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. Or follow the steps at https://getreturnsafe.com/delete-account.
